EffectReceipt is not reward. Operational success, desired outcome and causal contribution are different claims.

## Scope rule

**The scope of evidence bounds the scope of learning.** One execution may justify execution-local adaptation; repeated comparable episodes may update a Project posterior; transportable evidence across relevant Projects may justify a project-class/global prior.

Global priors help cold start; local evidence should override when strong enough. Measure negative transfer rather than assuming similarity.

## Feedback

Workers/reviewers/humans can report that context, tools, workflows or controls are poor. Treat feedback as a sensor/claim linked to trajectory and corroboration, not learned truth.

## Procedural evolution

Useful recurring structure may evolve from experience → procedure candidate → governed workflow → deterministic machinery. Better learning can decrease LLM/agent usage.

## Self-evolution constraints

Candidate cannot own sole evaluator or promotion authority. Evaluator changes require metrology. Use progressive evidence: replay, held-out, shadow, canary, rollback. Preserve rejected candidates/negative evidence enough to avoid rediscovery loops.

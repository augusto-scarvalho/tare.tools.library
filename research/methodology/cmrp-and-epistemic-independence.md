## What the experiment found

A same-model cyclic role process (planner, archaeologist, literature/bleeding-edge scouts, source curator, evidence analyst, architecture reconciler, experimental designer, adversarial reviewer, revision delta, final audit) improved discipline by externalizing intermediate artifacts and forcing explicit falsification.

But it did **not** create independent agents/evidence. Same-model roles share model biases, source selection and causal context.

```text
same-model sequential roles
 = structured test-time research compute
 + provenance discipline
 + explicit adversarial passes
 ≠ independent EvidenceFamily
```

Same-model final audit is screening, not strict independent proof.

## Important adversarial findings

Role labels may create false independence; performance may come simply from more compute; shared context can destroy functional isolation; self-correction can degrade correct answers; literature selection and interpretation can be circular.

## Qualification experiment

Compare compute-matched conditions: single-pass, generic iterative baseline, same-model CMRP, same-model isolated-context roles, heterogeneous models, heterogeneous + independent/human review. Cross shared-context versus artifact-only handoff.

Measure supported-claim precision, contradiction recall, negative-evidence preservation, source-identity correctness, false-correction rate, architectural drift, adversarial yield, residual unsupported claims, calibration and cost-to-trust.

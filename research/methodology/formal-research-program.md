# Formal Research Program — preservation edition

**Status:** RESEARCH methodology lineage originating 2026-07-14.

## Goal

Study adaptive, governed, project-oriented multi-agent/Agent-OS architecture with enough methodological discipline to produce architecture, experiments, benchmarks and falsifiable evidence rather than only implementation anecdotes.

## Mixed-method program

- Systematic Mapping to identify communities, terms, gaps and benchmarks.
- Focused SLRs with transparent selection, snowballing and updates.
- Design Science Research for artifact construction/evaluation.
- Longitudinal case studies including tare.tools itself and heterogeneous external Projects.
- Controlled/quasi-experiments for routing, workflows, reviewers, memory and evolution.
- ATAM for architecture tradeoffs.
- Goal–Question–Metric for operational measurement.

## Evidence hierarchy

Separate seminal/spec/formal foundations, peer-reviewed surveys/benchmarks, reproducible frontier preprints, vendor engineering and community signals. Important architectural claims should be triangulated rather than supported only by vendor/community material.

## Research objects

Preserve protocol, unit of analysis, candidate/subject identity, evaluator identity, repeats/randomization where relevant, exclusions, stopping rules, delayed outcomes, negative evidence and cost.

## Threats

Data contamination, benchmark overfitting, same-model circularity, vendor drift, hidden compute, non-independent judges, project-specific selection bias and changing architecture epoch.

## Output chain

Research → Findings → ADRs → Canonical Architecture → SPEC → BDD → Implementation Packet → Code/Gates → Outcome Evidence → new Research.

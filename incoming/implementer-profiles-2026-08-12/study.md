## 1. Question and unit of analysis

The study asks how much implementation autonomy can be delegated before audit burden, rework, semantic overclaim, architecture drift or effect risk outweigh throughput gains.

The unit is contextual:

```text
ImplementerProfile =
  Model × Runtime × Effort × Capsule × WorkFormat
  × Tool/Capability Surface × Isolation × Authority Envelope
  × Repository State × Verification Regime × Audit Relationship × Task Class
```

Therefore Role, autonomy treatment, observed capability, Authority, qualification, evidence independence and economics are separate axes.

## 2. A0–A6: origin and meaning

The ladder is an **authorial tare.tools Design Science artifact** created before the extended literature review:

Literature on levels of automation, adjustable autonomy/delegation, organizational roles, RBAC, trust in automation, validity arguments and assurance cases provides construct ancestry and convergent support; it is not claimed as the origin of the seven levels.

## 5. Current profile and failure modes

Evidence-bounded description:

> **High-throughput bounded implementer with qualified A2 DEV decomposition, strong correctibility and scope/effect discipline, but persistent semantic/evidence edge risk that makes independent audit materially valuable.**

Strengths: decomposition, throughput, incumbent composition, bounded self-correction, negative-evidence preservation and resistance to several unsafe shortcuts.

Persistent risks: state/ownership/provenance edges, local semantic closure, terminal-state imprecision, false concreteness/nominal compliance and nonzero auditor/corrective burden.

The successful operating pattern is better described as:

```text
contract → bounded implementation → independent falsification
→ bounded corrective → independent settlement
```

than as one-shot `task → done`.

## 7. Cost-to-trust

Primary outcome:

> **total expenditure until an independently qualifiable candidate exists.**

Measure outcome severity, corrective count, reproducibility, owner interruptions, Authority/scope discipline, auditor effort, elapsed time, runner/model cost and **correctibility** from falsification to accepted closure.

Working hypothesis: a cheap bounded implementer plus strong independent assurance may beat more expensive one-shot reasoning on cost-to-trust. Matched telemetry is still insufficient to establish this.

## 8. Validity and claim ceiling

- **Scoring:** Q7/Q8 behavior is directly observable in pre-mutation decomposition and independent audits; auditor-designed envelopes create researcher coupling.
- **Generalization:** two formal A2 probes plus naturalistic support exceed anecdote, but remain same project/runtime/capsule and close in time.
- **Extrapolation:** CI-REGRESSION-01 is positive Validation/Assurance transfer evidence, not a matched cross-class qualification.
- **Implications:** only bounded DEV A2 under the observed regime is justified.

Not supported: A3, global default A2, protected merge/promotion Authority, autonomous activation, self-audit, cross-project qualification or vendor-wide superiority.

1. Scope, provenance and freeze point

This edition turns the accumulated implementer study into a publication-shaped artifact for tare.tools.research. The unit of analysis is not the model alone, but the full operational profile: Model × Runtime × Effort × Capsule × WorkFormat × Tool/Capability Surface × Isolation × Authority Envelope × Repository State × Verification Regime × Audit Relationship × Task Class.

Draft freeze. The corpus is frozen at the first audited RESULT of F5L-06 / RELAY-Q7, candidate 65ca9eb7a8bd569f1260ac433194a89dd791a337. The corrective on Issue #11 is still running and must be incorporated before final merge/publication.

Authority: EXPERIMENTAL / RESEARCH. This document does not promote any profile, runtime, or behavior to TARGET/CURRENT.

2. Research problem

The operational question is: how much implementation autonomy can be delegated to an ImplementerProfile, per task class, before audit cost, rework, semantic overclaim, or architecture drift outweigh throughput gains?

The working hypothesis is that freedom over means can increase progressively while Authority, protected effects, canonical promotion, and architecture ownership remain deterministic and external to the implementer.

3. Longitudinal method and causal limits

The corpus combines historical Opus 4.8 High sessions, a long Sonnet 5 xhigh epoch, and a Fable 5 low epoch with progressive A0→A1→A2 probing. These episodes are not a clean causal A/B: packet size, prompts, authority/effect ceilings, isolation, harness maturity, and audit regime changed as well.

Epistemic rule. This study produces qualification evidence, not a causal leaderboard. Results are task-class-specific and must be interpreted as longitudinal evidence under known confounds.

The ladder measures an engineering decision surface. No level grants Authority over protected effects, policy, credentials, promotion, main/staging, rulesets, or canonical architecture change.

Fable 5 low has a positive A2 decomposition signal, but A2 is not yet qualified: the Q7 corrective remains open.

7. Requirement/architecture conformance under YAGNI pressure

A separate axis was introduced after observing the Fable × Ponytail interaction. The risk is not only refusal. A subtler failure is to locally represent an architectural property as satisfied while the binding, receipt, or invariant that gives it system-wide meaning is absent.

Authority / Policy / effect ceiling
        ↓
Canonical Architecture / ADR / SPEC / BDD
        ↓
Frozen acceptance claims
        ↓
CURRENT reconciliation
        ↓
YAGNI / KISS / DRY / Ponytail heuristics

F5L-04 produced the desired behavior: the implementer challenged a requirement as potentially YAGNI but still implemented the frozen contract. Causality between Fable and Ponytail remains OPEN.

8. Primary metric: cost-to-trust

Green tests alone are insufficient. The recommended metric is cost-to-trust: total expenditure until an independently qualifiable candidate exists.

Outcome / assurance

audit verdict, finding severity, corrective-train count, fresh-clone reproducibility, evidence acceptance.

Governance / scope

authority escalations, unexpected effects, silent scope cuts, unauthorized deferrals, architecture substitutions.

Autonomy

packet decomposition quality, self-discovered defects, plan revisions, challenge-without-substitution.

Economics / control plane

human interruptions, coordination token proxies, deterministic/API/model control actions, elapsed time where reliably observed.

# Peer-review protocol — bounded implementer profiles

**Status:** `RESEARCH / REVIEW PROTOCOL`  
**Purpose:** define how a reviewer can accept, weaken or falsify the study's classification claims without relying on the authors' favorable narrative.

## 1. Study design

Treat the work as a combination of:

1. **longitudinal embedded case study** — repeated implementation episodes in one evolving software project;
2. **Design Science artifact evaluation** — A0–A6 is an authorial operational taxonomy;
3. **progressive qualification experiment program** — autonomy treatments are increased prospectively under a frozen Authority/evidence envelope;
4. **naturalistic transfer observation** — operational trains outside formal probes can test transferability but do not become retrospective qualification trials.

It is not a general vendor/model benchmark and not a causal randomized trial.

## 2. Independent coding requirement

Before using A0–A6 for broader claims, appoint at least one reviewer who did not create the rubric.

The reviewer receives:
- the frozen A0–A6 behavior anchors;
- packet/evidence records without the original final A-level label where feasible;
- exact GitHub Issue/Git identities;
- explicit distinction between implementer report and independent audit.

The reviewer independently codes:
- treatment actually granted;
- behaviors observed;
- evidence class;
- qualification implication, if any;
- contradictory evidence.

Agreement may be summarized with an appropriate inter-rater statistic only after the coding data exists. No agreement coefficient is claimed in this submission.

## 3. Validity argument

Each qualification claim should be challenged in four stages.

### Scoring

Questions:
- Did the episode actually expose the decision latitude attributed to the treatment?
- Was the behavior produced before mutation/result, or reconstructed favorably afterward?
- Is the observation bound to exact candidate/evidence?
- Did the auditor prescribe the behavior that is being claimed as autonomous?

### Generalization

Questions:
- Does the behavior repeat across episodes?
- Are task classes meaningfully distinct?
- Are failures/correctives retained in the sample?
- Is qualification robust to minor repo/capsule changes?

### Extrapolation

Questions:
- Does bounded DEV performance transfer to Validation/Assurance, Runtime, protected promotion or another repository?
- Is the same effect observed under another runtime/effort treatment?
- Does the implementation-audit loop remain affordable at higher risk?

### Implications

Questions:
- What routing/reliance decision is justified?
- Is uncertainty/scope preserved?
- Has anyone incorrectly converted qualification into Authority?

A claim fails if its implications exceed the evidence even when scoring is strong.

## 4. Claim ceilings for this edition

Reviewer may accept at most:

```text
Fable 5 low × Claude Code
× observed tare.tools capsule
× bounded DEV implementation
× independent audit
→ A2 bounded DEV qualified
```

and:

```text
CI-REGRESSION-01
→ positive naturalistic A2 transfer signal into Validation/Assurance
```

The following are explicitly unsupported:
- A3+ qualification;
- global/default A2;
- self-promotion or protected Authority;
- causal Fable > Sonnet/Opus claim;
- cross-project qualification;
- economic superiority without matched telemetry.

## 5. Taxonomy falsifiers

Revise, merge levels or retire A0–A6 if one or more of these persist under independent replication:

1. independent reviewers cannot reliably distinguish adjacent levels from episode evidence;
2. matched experiments do not show different behavior when delegated latitude changes;
3. A-level does not predict any useful downstream outcome such as corrective burden, owner interruption or cost-to-trust;
4. a smaller taxonomy explains the observations equally well with less ambiguity;
5. higher autonomy systematically increases architecture/Authority defects without compensating trust/cost benefit;
6. classification mostly tracks task difficulty or auditor prompt detail rather than delegated autonomy.

## 6. Next matched experiments

### E1 — Fable low vs Sonnet xhigh

Freeze:
- exact repository subject;
- identical bounded task;
- same capsule/work format/tool surface;
- same Authority/effect ceiling;
- same verification and independent auditor.

Primary outcomes:
- independently accepted/not accepted;
- severity/count of findings;
- corrective iterations;
- owner interruptions;
- elapsed implementation + audit time;
- model/runner cost when auditable;
- evidence completeness;
- architecture/Authority violations.

### E2 — Fable low vs higher effort

Same design, changing effort treatment only where the runtime exposes it consistently. This isolates whether low effort's apparent cost advantage survives audit burden.

### E3 — formal A2 transfer probe in Validation/Assurance

The objective and Authority envelope are frozen by the auditor, but the implementer must generate its own bounded decomposition. CI-REGRESSION-01 is useful naturalistic evidence but should not be relabeled retrospectively as this formal probe.

### E4 — A3 falsifier co-design

Only after repeated A2 transfer evidence. The auditor supplies the objective and invariants but intentionally does **not** pre-specify material falsifiers. Qualification requires the implementer to originate tests capable of disproving its preferred design.

## 7. Threats to validity

- **Researcher/auditor coupling:** the same ChatGPT lineage designed treatments and performed many audits.
- **Instrumentation drift:** harness, relay, tests and evidence conventions improved during the study.
- **Carry-over:** Fable may benefit from artifacts created during Sonnet/Opus epochs.
- **Task mismatch:** historical profiles did not execute identical tasks.
- **Survivorship/selection bias:** successful or interesting trains may be better documented.
- **Provider/runtime confounding:** model, runtime, capsule and effort changed together in several transitions.
- **Small-N:** only two formal A2 probes currently establish bounded DEV qualification.
- **Ecological concentration:** most evidence comes from one repository/project.
- **Audit as treatment component:** results cannot be attributed solely to implementer cognition because independent falsification materially changes outcomes.

## 8. Peer-review acceptance checklist

A reviewer should verify:

- [ ] A0–A6 origin is described as authorial, not falsely literature-derived.
- [ ] autonomy and Authority are separated.
- [ ] Q7/Q8 exact audited outcomes support the bounded A2 claim.
- [ ] CI-REGRESSION-01 is represented as settled naturalistic support, not a formal third A2 probe.
- [ ] CI-REGRESSION-02 is represented as running/ACK-only at this freeze.
- [ ] negative evidence and corrective loops are visible.
- [ ] Sonnet/Opus are not retroactively over-coded.
- [ ] cost-to-trust remains a measurement objective/hypothesis where telemetry is incomplete.
- [ ] publication is not represented as tare.tools architecture ratification.

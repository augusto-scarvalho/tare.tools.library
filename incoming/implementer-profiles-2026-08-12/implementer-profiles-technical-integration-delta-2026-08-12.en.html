4. Router rules

  • Reputation/qualification may filter or rank otherwise eligible candidates.
  • It cannot make a runtime eligible when Authority/policy/capability forbids it.
  • Qualification should be task-class-specific and may decay after model/runtime/harness change.
  • Semantic and evidence-completion failures should be scored separately from throughput.

5. Autonomy calibration

A0–A6 should remain treatment metadata, with promotion/demotion based on repeated episodes and independent audit. Silent YAGNI cuts, architecture substitution, and false-concreteness are demotion signals.

7. Anti-drift

  • Do not introduce a kernel-wide ImplementerScore before demonstrated need.
  • Do not merge Model, Provider, Runtime, Executor Lane, and Commercial Lane.
  • Do not treat autonomy level as a Permit.
  • Do not treat research publication as CURRENT/TARGET promotion.

Proposal: Research Lineage, Influence & Provenance in Documents

A generated relationship capsule for scientific research and technical companions.

Relationship metadata is evidence/provenance. It does not create architectural authority or prove causation unless the edge evidence says so.

1. Goal

Make ancestry and cross-document influence visible inside each document without duplicating or hand-maintaining a second truth.

2. Proposed section

Research Lineage, Influence & Provenance
- Historical ancestors
- Direct derivatives
- Internal corpus anchors
- Cross-lineage interference
- External source families
- Downstream influence
- Authority note

3. Source semantics

A citation is factual. Support/challenge scope is curated. Co-citation is not lineage. Paper-to-paper succession is only recorded when explicit evidence establishes it.

4. Rollout

  1. Add graph/schema/indexes as read-only metadata.
  2. Generate relationship capsule for the 20 current refresh documents in shadow.
  3. Validate links and parity.
  4. Adopt the section in the next editorial standard revision.
  5. Backfill historical editorial editions only as generated metadata, never by rewriting originals.

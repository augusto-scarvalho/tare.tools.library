## 1. What was actually done by reasoning

The curation decision was made over **knowledge lineages**, not files. Historical documents, 2026-08-11 refreshes, 2026-08-10/12 deep-research packs, empirical falsifications and the tare.tools North Star were compared to identify:

- surviving claims/findings;
- contradictions and negative evidence;
- historical concepts subsumed by canonical equivalents;
- unresolved questions;
- experiments that still carry independent empirical value;
- repeated boilerplate that no longer justified a live document.

No deterministic script was used to decide equivalence, survival, supersession, ADOPT/ADAPT/RETIRE/OPEN status or the new eight-study decomposition.

## 3. Content-quality checks performed manually

- **No duplicate ownership:** each study has a distinct primary question even where causal chains cross contexts.
- **No TARGET inflation:** OPEN research remains OPEN; studies do not mint CURRENT/TARGET.
- **Negative evidence preserved:** static parity false-green, manual-proof minting, weak test-count logic, parked synthetic Memory Caching and same-model non-independence survive as findings.
- **No mega-document collapse:** Reliability/effects and Governance/Assurance remain separable despite sharing evidence/lineage.
- **No archive-by-anxiety:** Git history is used for raw historical recovery instead of duplicating an `/archive` tree.
- **Selected bibliography:** sources are retained because they support/challenge specific findings, with limitations; source count is not a quality metric.

## 4. Known limitations of this candidate

1. **External freshness was not re-verified source-by-source during this semantic pass.** The evidence registry is inherited from previously researched lineages. Any implementation/normative promotion depending on current vendor/spec behavior must re-check the relevant primary source.
2. **The eight living studies are intentionally syntheses, not replacements for every detail ever written.** Deep historical argumentation remains in Git/File Library and should be consulted when a claim is disputed or promoted.
3. **Implementer Profiles** appears worthy of a dedicated empirical study but was not ingested blindly because its episode ledger still requires reconciliation.
4. **RNN recurrent-memory** remains a separate lab result and must not be imported into Agent OS architecture by analogy.

Research metadata · 2026-08-11

Research Lineage, Influence & Source Graph

A conservative provenance graph connecting historical documents, refreshed lineages, technical deltas, papers, repositories and standards.

Relationship metadata is evidence/provenance. It does not create architectural authority or prove causation unless the edge evidence says so.

1. Model

Lineage and influence are distinct. Lineage captures derivation/version/evidence ancestry; influence captures how one body of work changes scope, confidence or architectural interpretation of another. Edges declare their basis and confidence.

Coverage: 93/93 historical artifacts, 20 refreshed documents, 58 external sources, 26 curated cross-lineage edges.

3. Paper / source relationships

External citations are recorded as observed CITED_BY edges. Co-citation is stored separately and never interpreted as descent. Potential same-work/version families remain candidates until verified, except explicit official families.

Machine-readable artifacts: RESEARCH_RELATION_GRAPH.json, SOURCE_CO_CITATION.json, SOURCE_FAMILIES.json.

4. Proposed addition to documents

Add a generated Research Lineage, Influence & Provenance section to every scientific research and technical companion. It should show ancestors, internal anchors, cross-lineage constraints/support, key external source families, downstream derivatives and an authority note.

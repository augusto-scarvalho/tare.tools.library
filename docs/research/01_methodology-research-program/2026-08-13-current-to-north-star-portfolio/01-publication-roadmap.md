## 1. Editorial principle

Not every implementation train becomes a paper. Publish when the episode produces a generalizable question, an identifiable treatment/contrast, sufficient provenance and negative evidence that allows falsification.

Internal operational evidence can start a case study, but must not be presented as a causal benchmark without matched controls/replication.

## 4. Line C — Negative evidence is not candidate regression

## 12. Release criteria for any external paper/draft

- provenance of internal evidence;
- reproducible/sanitized artifacts or clear disclosure of access limitation;
- negative results;
- CURRENT×TARGET labels preserved;
- threats to validity;
- second review when the author was also auditor/designer;
- primary and bleeding-edge bibliography kept separate;
- no causal vendor/model leaderboard without a matched experiment;
- no architecture claim promoted by recency of the research document.

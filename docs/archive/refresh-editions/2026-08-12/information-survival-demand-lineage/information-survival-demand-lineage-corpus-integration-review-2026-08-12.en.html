4. Scientific review

The review preserves the pack's conclusions while narrowing overclaims. RETRACE supplies an independent semantic signal via bidirectional reconstruction, not proof authority. RepoProbe supports edit-bias and atomic-fact/checklist evaluation, not the sufficiency of any local checklist. EA-Graph supports separating evidence strength from freshness in a bounded testbed; it does not establish universal agent-memory behavior. SWE-RPG supports intermediate evidence for clarification/planning without ratifying Demand as a kernel primitive.

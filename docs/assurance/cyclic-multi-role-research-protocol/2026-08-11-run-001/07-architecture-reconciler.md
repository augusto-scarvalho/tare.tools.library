# Role 07 — Architecture / Method Reconciler

**Run:** CMRP-2026-08-11-001

## CURRENT × TARGET × PROPOSED × RESEARCH

### CURRENT
- this ChatGPT runtime can execute sequential role passes but cannot spawn general independent subagents;
- tare.tools.research already supports immutable artifacts, lineage, Research Frontier, source/evidence metadata and separate promotion authority;
- no canonical Agent OS primitive for research roles is required or proven.

### TARGET
Research remains evidence. Any future tare-owned execution should use canonical Workflow/Runtime/Capability/Evidence contracts and keep promotion authority separate.

### PROPOSED
Adopt an **executor-neutral Cyclic Multi-Role Research Protocol (CMRP)** inside `tare.tools.research` as a research methodology/workflow profile, initially in shadow/experimental operation.

### RESEARCH
Optimal role count, cycle depth, context-isolation strategy and cost/quality trade-off remain empirical questions.

## ADOPT / ADAPT / RETIRE / OPEN

### ADOPT
- explicit role missions and forbidden moves;
- frozen intermediate artifacts;
- source/evidence passes before architecture synthesis;
- explicit contradiction/falsifier search;
- adversarial review followed by visible revision delta;
- final authority note;
- provenance stating `same-model sequential role` and `NOT_INDEPENDENT`.

### ADAPT
- “multi-agent research” → executor-neutral workflow that may run same-model sequentially or true multi-agent later;
- “reviewer independence” → separate **functional role separation**, **cognitive diversity**, **epistemic independence** and **authority independence**;
- “self-reflection” → evidence-guided critique with external/deterministic feedback where possible;
- “stop when satisfied” → bounded cycles with explicit evidence-delta/stopping criteria.

### RETIRE
- counting same-model role passes as multiple EvidenceFamilies;
- persona theatrics as a proxy for diversity;
- majority voting/consensus as proof;
- same-model final audit as strict proof authority;
- silent rewriting of earlier role outputs.

### OPEN
- whether fresh-context passes materially reduce correlated error;
- optimal number/order of roles;
- when heterogeneous model review has sufficient marginal value;
- whether blinded/independent search queries improve contradiction recall;
- how much cost is justified per research-risk class.

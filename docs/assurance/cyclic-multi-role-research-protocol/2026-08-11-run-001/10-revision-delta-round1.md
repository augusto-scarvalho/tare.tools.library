## Revised protocol invariants

1. **Functional separation ≠ epistemic independence.**
2. **Role name does not grant qualification.**
3. **External evidence beats unsupported introspection.**
4. **Critique must identify a claim, defect/evidence and required action.**
5. **Correct prior outputs are protected from recency-only overwrite.**
6. **Every material revision leaves a delta.**
7. **Cycles terminate on evidence/change criteria, not self-satisfaction.**
8. **Same-model audit has a declared assurance ceiling.**
9. **Strict/high-stakes conclusions require independent deterministic, heterogeneous or human evidence appropriate to the claim.**
10. **The method stays executor-neutral so true subagents can later replace sequential roles without changing the research contract.**

5. Scientific and engineering evidence — 2026 refresh

AgentGUI [R01] provides unusually timely empirical support for long-running-agent supervision: trace visualization reduced time to locate key trace elements, and automated drift prevention improved small-agent completion in preliminary experiments.

Plover [R02] makes plans visible and locally editable while preserving progress, aligning closely with Workflow revision/GraphPatch rather than restart-oriented steering.

Terminal Is All You Need [R03] reframes terminal success in terms of representational compatibility, transparency and participation barriers. These are properties future GUI surfaces must preserve rather than aesthetics to copy.

Graph-of-Trace [R04] supports causal/structural visualizations over raw log walls. OTel [R05] can feed projections, but canonical evidence and authority should remain behind the UI.

Long-horizon web-agent benchmark generation [R06] suggests UX testing should be journey/state based with executable trajectories and deterministic validation, not screenshot fidelity alone.

E2The proposed control/optimization does not merely move failures to hidden dimensions.Adversarial/held-out conditions plus delayed outcome review.EvidenceFamily-aware false negatives, incidents, rollbacks, residual risk.

Every experiment should record architecture epoch, candidate identity, evaluator identity/qualification, causal IDs, exclusions, failures and negative evidence. Statistical treatment should match the design: paired confidence intervals or bootstrap for repeated task suites, survival/time-to-event when latency/recovery matters, and calibration/regret metrics for prediction/routing problems.

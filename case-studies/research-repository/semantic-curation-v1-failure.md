# Semantic Curation V1 — over-compression case study

**Status:** DOGFOOD NEGATIVE EVIDENCE.

V1 correctly removed many mechanical projections but then compressed the surviving intellectual corpus into eight small syntheses. The result preserved many top-level theses while losing too much argumentation, bibliography, experimental method, alternatives and ability for a future reader to disagree with the synthesis.

## Falsified assumption

“Related thesis ⇒ merge documents” is insufficient.

## Replacement rule

Merge only when the successor preserves material questions, evidence, arguments, contradictions, experiments, limitations, bibliography/provenance and open branches. Optimize for **few useless files**, not few files.

## Repository-level lesson

Synthesis is a layer above deep studies. Git is the archaeological archive; HEAD is the living study surface. Those two facts do not justify making HEAD shallow.

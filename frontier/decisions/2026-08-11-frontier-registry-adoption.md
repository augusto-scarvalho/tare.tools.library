# Research Frontier Registry — Adoption Decision

**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** ACCEPTED_FOR_EXPERIMENTAL_OPERATION_IN_RESEARCH_REPOSITORY

The project owner accepted the Research Frontier model for organized handling of pointers left across chats and documents.

This acceptance authorizes implementation of the **research metadata/workflow inside `tare.tools.research`**. It does not ratify new Agent OS primitives, architecture, roadmap priority or implementation tasks.

## Accepted operating model

- preserve pointer origin/context;
- distinguish Pointer → Research Question → Hypothesis → Study → Finding;
- preserve lifecycle rather than OPEN/CLOSED only;
- cluster without deleting specificity;
- harvest explicit pointer surfaces automatically;
- keep promotion/merge semantic curation conservative;
- expose Radar/Digest as projections only;
- preserve `why_not_now`, resolution and reopen triggers when curated later;
- integrate an `Open Research Frontier` section in shadow relationship capsules before making it mandatory in all research documents.

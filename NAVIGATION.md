## 4. Evidence-driven continuation

When you finish a study, choose the next layer deliberately:

- **Need the implementation hypothesis?** → [Technical Proposals](proposals/README.md)
- **Need real observed failures/results?** → [Experiments](experiments/README.md) and [Case Studies](case-studies/README.md)
- **Need the current supported conclusions?** → [Curated Findings](findings/CURATED_FINDINGS.md)
- **Need unresolved work?** → [Research Frontier](frontier/RESEARCH_FRONTIER.md)
- **Need source provenance / exact HTML status?** → [Sources & Provenance](sources/README.md)
- **Need to understand why a historical document disappeared?** → [Curation Catalog](catalog/README.md)

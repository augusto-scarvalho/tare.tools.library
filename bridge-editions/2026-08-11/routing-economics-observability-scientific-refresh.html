5. Scientific and engineering evidence — 2026 refresh

LLMRouterBench [R01] is a corrective to router hype: sophisticated methods often cluster together and some fail to beat simple baselines, while the oracle gap persists. tare should preserve simple baselines and demand incremental value.

Agent-as-a-Router [R02] and SWE-Router [R03] both show that execution experience/partial trajectory can be more informative than task text alone. This strongly supports routing as an evidence-connected loop rather than a one-shot classifier.

RouteJudge [R04] makes router-level records explicit—decision, response, cost, latency, preference—which maps well to tare's causal evidence requirements.

Correlation-aware bandits [R05] and early-experience routing [R06] provide plausible research directions for bounded exploration and cold start, but they must remain behind shadow/OPE/canary and cannot own promotion authority.

Current OTel GenAI telemetry [R07] can provide cost/latency/token signals, but the canonical RouteDecision/OutcomeEvidence chain must remain tare-owned.
